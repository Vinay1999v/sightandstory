FAQ

Good questions deserve clear answers.

How much does a website cost?

Every quote is shaped by scope. Page count, custom features, e-commerce, content, photography, integrations and timing all affect the proposal. A discovery conversation comes first, followed by a written scope.

How long does a website take to complete?

A focused website may take several weeks; a larger brand, photography and website engagement takes longer. Your proposal includes milestones and makes content, feedback and approval responsibilities clear.

Do you work with new businesses?

Yes. Sight & Story can help shape the name, positioning, visual identity, content and website for a business building its first professional presence.

Can you redesign an existing website?

Yes. The process begins with an audit of the current message, content, usability, search foundations and performance so the redesign solves the right problems.

Do you provide written content and photography?

Content guidance and original business or product photography can be included. Model coordination is also available when the concept genuinely needs it.

Can you create a logo and full brand identity?

Yes. Identity work can include a logo system, color, typography, voice direction and practical guidelines for consistent use.

Will my website work on mobile devices?

Yes. Layouts and interactions are designed and tested for phones, tablets and desktops rather than treated as a desktop design squeezed smaller.

Is SEO included?

Every website includes sound technical and on-page foundations. Broader SEO strategy, ongoing content and local search work can be added to the scope.

Can you manage the website after launch?

Yes. Maintenance, content updates, analytics reviews, search improvements and campaign support can be arranged as ongoing support.

Who owns the website after completion?

Ownership, source files, platform access and third-party licenses are defined in the agreement. The handoff is designed to be clear, documented and usable.

Do you provide domain and hosting assistance?

Yes. Sight & Story can recommend and connect appropriate services. Third-party fees and account ownership remain clearly separated from the project quote.
